Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2, 2026

1. Commitment to Privacy

At Footage (developed under MemoryCurator), your privacy is our core engineering foundation. Footage is built from the ground up to operate 100% on your local device. We do not operate remote photo-processing servers, cloud AI services, or remote analytics platforms for photo content.

2. Data Collection and Transmission

We collect ZERO personal data, photos, videos, or biometrics.

  • No Photo Uploads: Your photos, videos, media metadata, EXIF parameters, and locations never leave your device.
  • No Facial Biometric Cloud Storage: On-device facial detection and clustering run locally using Android ML Kit. No facial embeddings or biometrics are transmitted anywhere.
  • No User Accounts: You do not need an account, sign-in, or registration to use Footage.

3. Device Permissions Requested

Footage requires certain Android system permissions strictly to perform local gallery functions:

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES & READ_MEDIA_VIDEO: Required to read photos and videos from your local device storage so the app can display and organize them.
  • ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION: Required to extract local GPS metadata from your photos for location-based timeline grouping.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used optionally to alert you on-device when fresh memories or new quality highlights are ready for review.

4. Third-Party Services

Footage utilizes on-device Google ML Kit and Android LiteRT libraries. These libraries execute locally within the application binary. No third-party tracking, advertising SDKs, or cloud analytics trackers are embedded into the app.

5. Children's Privacy

Footage does not collect or transmit personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
