Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 3, 2026 • Developed by Shivayu Consultants

1. Commitment to Privacy & Digital Integrity

At Footage (developed under Shivayu Consultants), your privacy and trust are our core engineering foundations. Footage is built from the ground up to operate 100% on your local device. We do not operate remote photo-processing servers, cloud AI services, or remote analytics platforms.

2. 100% Free • Zero Ads • Zero Trackers Guarantee

Footage is completely free of charge. We guarantee:

  • No In-App Advertising: We do not embed ad networks (e.g., AdMob, Unity Ads) or pop-ups.
  • No Behavioral Trackers: We do not collect telemetry, usage behavior, or user analytics.
  • No Hidden Costs or Subscriptions: Every feature is unlocked and completely free forever. No paywalls or microtransactions.

3. Data Collection and Transmission

We collect ZERO personal data, photos, videos, or biometrics.

  • No Photo Uploads: Your photos, videos, media metadata, EXIF parameters, and locations never leave your device sandbox.
  • No Facial Biometric Cloud Storage: On-device facial detection and clustering run locally on your phone. No facial embeddings or biometrics are transmitted anywhere.
  • No User Accounts: You do not need an account, sign-in, or registration to use Footage.

4. Device Permissions Requested

Footage requires certain Android system permissions strictly to perform local gallery functions:

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES & READ_MEDIA_VIDEO: Required to read photos and videos from your local device storage so the app can display and organize them.
  • ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION: Required to extract local GPS metadata from your photos for location-based timeline grouping.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Used optionally to alert you on-device when fresh memories or new quality highlights are ready for review.

5. Third-Party Services

Footage executes code locally within the application binary. No third-party tracking, advertising SDKs, or cloud analytics trackers are embedded into the app.

6. Children's Privacy

Footage does not collect or transmit personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
