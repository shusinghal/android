Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 17, 2026 • Published by Shivayu Consultants

1. Core Privacy Commitment

Footage is engineered with a strict zero-data-collection architecture. We believe personal photography should remain completely private. All photo analysis, aesthetic scoring, burst detection, and cleanup recommendations execute 100% locally on your smartphone. We do not operate remote photo-processing servers, cloud AI pipelines, or central databases.

2. Data Collection & Transmission

We do not collect, transmit, or monetize your personal content:

  • Zero Photo Uploads: Your images, videos, thumbnails, and EXIF metadata never leave your device.
  • No Facial Biometric Cloud Storage: On-device facial detection and clustering run locally inside your app's sandbox. No face embeddings or identity profiles are transmitted to any server.
  • No User Accounts: You are never asked to create an account, register an email address, or provide personal credentials to use the application.

3. Android System Permissions

To function as a local gallery and photo curator, Footage requests standard Android runtime permissions strictly for local execution:

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES & READ_MEDIA_VIDEO: Grants local access to index and display photos on your device storage without modifying originals without your instruction.
  • ACCESS_MEDIA_LOCATION: Used locally to read GPS coordinates embedded in EXIF tags for chronological and geographical album grouping.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: (Optional) Used solely to notify you when on-device processing of a burst batch has finished.

4. Third-Party Trackers & Advertising

Footage contains zero third-party advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob or Unity Ads) and zero analytics frameworks. We do not monitor user habits, track device identifiers (IMEI/AAID), or share data with data brokers.

5. Photo Deletion & Storage Management

When you choose to clean pre-sorted bad takes or duplicate bursts, Footage utilizes Android MediaStore and Storage Access Framework APIs to execute the file action directly on your local device. Deleted files are handled according to your device's system trash policies.

6. Google Play Data Safety Compliance

In accordance with Google Play Data Safety requirements, Footage declares that no user data is shared with third parties, no data is collected, and all operations occur strictly on the user's local hardware.

7. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

Footage does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 years of age.
